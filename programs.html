What we do, in detail

Four services, with the patch test rule applied to every colour without exception.

Program 01 · Tested 48 hours first

Colour and highlights

The test is non-negotiable and we lose bookings over it, usually from people with an event the next day. Oxidative dyes cause genuine allergic reactions and occasionally dangerous ones, and the forty-eight hour test is written into every manufacturer's instructions for that reason. We also retest when the product changes, because a client tested on one brand is not tested on another.

  • Per service
  • Adults, minors with consent
  • Valid patch test
  • Condition permitting

Program 02 · Cut to the hair you have

Cutting and styling

A cut that only works for twenty minutes after leaving a salon is not a good cut. We ask what you actually do each morning and how much time you have, because a style that needs forty minutes of work will be abandoned by the second week. Honest about which reference photographs are achievable on your hair type and which are not.

  • Per service
  • All clients
  • Consultation first
  • No chemicals involved

Program 03 · Sometimes the answer is wait

Condition and repair

A great deal of damaged hair arrives wanting another chemical service to fix the last one. Often the honest answer is to stop, cut back gradually and let it recover, which no salon enjoys saying because it sells nothing that day. We will tell you when hair is at the point where more lightening will break it rather than lift it.

  • Per service
  • All clients
  • Assessment first
  • No guarantee of rescue

Program 04 · Free, including the test

Consultations and referral

Consultations are free and so is the patch test, because charging for the safety step would discourage it. We also do not diagnose: a scalp condition, unexplained hair loss, or any lesion or infection goes to a GP, pharmacist or dermatologist. A salon offering to treat a scalp condition is working outside what it is competent or insured to do.

  • Free
  • All clients
  • No obligation
  • Referral if clinical
